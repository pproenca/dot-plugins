# Recursive discovery

## Frame a working scope

Record system identity/version, time, business purpose, actors, configurations, regions, integrations, data-history eras, and observables relevant to the question. Missing dimensions are questions. Start broad enough to expose cross-boundary consequences; freeze a scoped snapshot only when reporting results.

Keep narratives and observation records in project-owned paths. Use synthetic identities in examples. Retain original evidence references and captures only where authorized. A snapshot of records can expose an exceptional state; the absence of another state does not prove it impossible.

## Alternate divergence and convergence

1. Collect stories independently from practitioners, source, product interaction, documents, and data. Keep an inventory-first investigator unaware of the current graph so disconnected behavior can be found.
2. Arrange meaningful events around a business story and terminal outcomes. Add people, systems, value, and hotspots. Do not equate a visually ordered story with a mandatory event order.
3. Walk through names and numbers. Add commands, decision information, policies, alternatives, and waits. Ask what always happens, what happens immediately, who can override it, and what happens when nobody acts.
4. Formalize stable discoveries as concepts and claims, preserving raw disagreements. Expand mandatory concept aspects and new domain-specific questions into the frontier.
5. Choose the next probe by uncertainty, consequence, cross-context reach, and cost. Use the least intrusive source capable of answering the question. Static extraction cannot establish an actual server rejection.
6. Challenge the resulting explanation, extract examples, verify where possible, and checkpoint the accepted slice. Repeat until the selected scope's obligations are satisfied or explicit blockers remain.

## Work allocation

One curator owns canonical edits. Workers receive separate proposal paths and the basis hash they researched. Reconcile proposals against the latest model; never let the last writer silently erase another perspective. Before merging, audit, compare scope and identifiers, preserve superseded history in a checkpoint/version-control diff, and audit again.

Partition by cohesive processes with deliberate cross-context review. A route-only partition misses background behavior; an entity-only partition misses journeys. Complement process investigators with source/contract, UI/experiment, data/history, inventory-first, and adversarial roles. Use the available concurrency limit; independence does not require a fixed swarm size or model name.

A downstream responsibility belongs in the investigation when its causal or business relevance is unresolved. It does not need to share a database transaction with the initiating action. Keep unknown handoffs open; investigate their relevance independently of atomicity.

An agent brief names:

- Question, business context, applicability envelope, and intended output.
- Available source categories and known access limits.
- Whether the agent should see the existing map or independently discover behavior.
- Evidence lineage, source locations, and counterexamples required in its response.
- File ownership and next handoff; no authority to mutate external systems beyond existing authorization.
- Bounded stop condition, such as answering one hypothesis or exhausting a specified source inventory.

## Avoid recursive busywork

Deduplicate by concept meaning within a context and scope, not by label alone. Link repeated concepts and loops; do not spawn another task for the same unresolved question. Retry a failed read once after checking the cause; after a second equivalent failure, record the blocker and move to independent work. Ambiguous writes require reconciliation before any retry.

N/A needs a precise reason and supporting evidence. A user-approved scope exclusion stays visible and separate from coverage. An agent running out of time creates an unfinished question, never an exclusion.

Represent delayed work, cancellation, timeouts, retry, duplication, concurrency, reversals, and manual handling only where applicable, but explicitly investigate applicability. Investigate reports, scheduled jobs, webhooks, imports/exports, flags, and historical states as well as screens and endpoints.

## Experiment record

Capture a testable hypothesis and competing explanation; exact environment, role, configuration and fixture; initial state; action; immediate result; delayed observations; persistent state and external effects; artifact location; limitations; and cleanup outcome. Record clocks and observation windows. A request accepted by a provider is not proof of delivery.

Do not infer production behavior from local replacement code. Do not treat generated API documentation and its originating schema as independent sources. Track their common lineage. Business intent may require a human decision even when runtime behavior is known.
